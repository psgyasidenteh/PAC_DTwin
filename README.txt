1. QUICK REVIEW WORKFLOW (RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To review the design deliverables efficiently:
  1. Report & PFD: Open PAC_Report.pdf (or .docx) and PAC_PFD.png for the plant overview.
  2. Slide Deck: Review Presentations/PAC_Presentation.pptx for executive summary.
  3. Economic Analysis: Inspect PAC_Economics_Final.xlsx (CAPEX, OPEX, Cash Flows).
  4. P&ID Flowsheets: Open the PDFs in "P&ID/" (convenient viewing without CAD software).
  5. Individual Designs: Check "Presentations/" and "Individual Drawings/" for unit designs.
  6. Process Simulation: Open the Aspen model in "PAC Aspen Simulation/".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DIRECTORY STRUCTURE & FILE DESCRIPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ROOT DIRECTORY ]
- PAC_Report.pdf / PAC_Report.docx
  * Comprehensive final plant design report (comprising background, site selection,
    material & energy balances, detailed equipment design, safety/HAZOP, plant
    layout, economics, and environmental impact).
  * Software: Adobe Acrobat / Browser (PDF) or Microsoft Word (DOCX).

- PAC_PFD.png / PAC_PFD.vsdx
  * Process Flow Diagram illustrating the complete plant layout from raw bauxite
    feed to final purified PAC solution.
  * Software: Any Image Viewer / Browser (PNG); Microsoft Visio / draw.io (VSDX).

- PAC_Economics_Final.xlsx
  * Complete financial model containing CAPEX, Labour, OPEX, and DCF Cash Flow sheets
    with NPV, IRR, and payback calculations.
  * Software: Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets / LibreOffice Calc.

- README.txt
  * This navigation and software access guide.


[ PAC Aspen Simulation/ ]
- PAC_Aspen_Simulation.bkp (Aspen Plus Backup File - RECOMMENDED)
- PAC_Aspen_Simulation.apw (Aspen Plus Compound Document)
- PAC_Aspen_Simulation.appdf, .xml, .his, .ads, .def
  * Software: Aspen Plus V14.0 (AspenTech).
  * Tip: Opening the .bkp file is recommended for best version compatibility.


[ P&ID/ (Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams) ]
Contains paired .pdf (for instant viewing) and .dwg (native CAD) files:
  - Area 100: Raw Material Handling and Preparation
  - Area 200: Pyro-Processing (Rotary Kiln)
  - Area 300: Flue Gas Treatment
  - Area 400: Dry Magnetic Separation
  - Area 500: Wet Grinding and Classification
  - Area 600: Leaching and Solid-Liquid Separation
  - Area 700: Basification, Polymerization and Purification
  * Software:
    - Quick Viewing: Any PDF Reader / Browser (.pdf). No CAD software required.
    - CAD Editing / Layer Inspection: AutoCAD or AutoCAD plant 3D (.dwg).


[ Presentations/ ]
- PAC_Presentation.pptx
  * Full group presentation covering the entire design scope, flowsheet, economics,
    and process control.
- Individual Presentations:
  * AFRIYIE, Melissa Darko.pptx        - Basicity Adjustment Reactor (CSTR)
  * AMOAH, Kelvin.pptx                 - Ball Mill Grinding Circuit (BM-101)
  * GYASI-DENTEH, Prince Sugar.pptx    - Reductive Roasting Rotary Kiln (RK-201)
  * OSABUTEY, Sylvester Nii Abbey.pptx - Two-Stage Jacketed CSTR Leaching System


[ Individual Drawings/ ]
- AFRIYIE, Melissa Darko.pdf        (Basicity Adjustment Reactor)
- AMOAH, Kelvin.pdf                 (Ball Mill BM-101)
- GYASI-DENTEH, Prince Sugar.pdf    (Rotary Kiln RK-201)
- OSABUTEY, Sylvester Nii Abbey.pdf (Two-Stage Jacketed Leaching CSTR)
  * Detailed engineering equipment fabrication and assembly drawings with nozzle
    schedules, mechanical dimensions, and bills of materials.
  * Software: Any PDF Reader (Adobe Acrobat, Edge, Chrome).
